Workflow

The previous script searched CDSE with a hard-coded box that missed the western part of the township, downloaded up to ten full GRD scenes from mixed tracks and orbit directions, and stopped there. The new workflow keeps the native 10 m grid from download to map.

    Data sources

    • Sentinel-1 IW GRDH, gamma0 RTC via CDSE Sentinel Hub (Copernicus DEM GLO-30 terrain flattening)
    • Sentinel-2 L2A via CDSE Sentinel Hub (Jan–Mar 2026 median NDVI; post-event 30 Sep / 5 Oct)
    • MIMU GeoNode: township, village tracts, villages, roads, rivers, schools, health facilities
    • Copernicus DEM GLO-30 and ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 (AWS open data)
    • OpenStreetMap (Overpass): buildings, roads, waterways
    • Open-Meteo historical weather API: daily rainfall 1991–2026
    • geonet-myanmar/launglon-landslides-2026: ten post-event drone inventories (2–8 Oct 2026), used for training and testing only
    Limits. One post-event pass (2 Oct, ascending) exists so far; no descending pass has been acquired since the event. Slopes facing away from the radar (east-facing, steep) are seen at grazing angles and are mapped less reliably. Landslides smaller than 0.2 ha are mostly missed, and runout on paddy can be confused with flooding. The map ranks where to look; it does not replace a field check.
